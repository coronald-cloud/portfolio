Behind the Cape.

I'm Ronald Conn III, an operator, artist, and systems builder based in Austin, Texas. I've spent years working inside companies as the person who just figured things out and then kept going. CRM builds, Python automation, 3D renders, video production, event design, staff training, inbox management. Whatever the moment required, I handled it.
